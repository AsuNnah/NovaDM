Privacy and security

Secure DNSLooks up sites over an encrypted connection. Fixes sites that open in other browsers but show a certificate error here.
Custom DNS-over-HTTPS addressFor example https://dns.nextdns.io/your-id
Block ads and trackersTurn it off for one site from the shield in the address bar.
Pop-ups and new windowsAsk: pop-ups and links that open another site in a new tab ask first. Block: pop-ups are blocked; links to other sites still ask. Ad-site pop-ups are always blocked.
Block third-party cookiesSites embedded in other sites (ads, trackers, "like" buttons) can't keep cookies there. A few embedded sign-ins and comment boxes may need you to open the site itself. Takes effect after NovaDM restarts.
Fingerprinting protectionStandard (like Brave): canvas and audio read-outs get tiny noise, different on every site. Strict (like Tor Browser): those read-outs are blocked, WebGL is off, and CPU, memory, screen and device APIs look the same for everyone; drawing apps, some games and some sites' “are you human” checks may not work. Turn it off for one site from the shield.
Security levelTor Browser's levels. Safer: no JIT compiler (scripts about 2× slower; WebAssembly off), JavaScript off on http:// sites, no web fonts, audio and video play when clicked, strict fingerprinting. Safest: JavaScript off everywhere; many sites break. The JIT part applies after NovaDM restarts and can't be turned off per site.
Use HTTPS when the site supports ithttp:// links open over an encrypted connection; sites without HTTPS open as before.
Skip tracking redirectsLinks that pass through a tracker first (google.com/url, l.facebook.com…) go straight to the page.
Remove tracking codes from addressesfbclid, gclid, msclkid and similar click identifiers.
Open the original page instead of AMPGoogle AMP copies of news pages open on the publisher's own site.
Warn about leaked passwordsWhen a site gets a password you type, NovaDM checks it against Have I Been Pwned's list of passwords from data breaches. Only the first 5 characters of a scrambled (hashed) form of it leave this PC, never the password. NovaDM also warns when a password is sent without HTTPS.
Warn about deceptive sitesStops pages on lists of phishing and malware sites (Phishing URL Blocklist, uBlock Badware risks), with a choice to continue anyway. The lists are downloaded once a day; the sites you visit are checked on this PC and never sent anywhere. Works even with Shields down.
Tell me when a new version is outOnce a day NovaDM asks GitHub which NovaDM release is the newest (nothing else is sent). A new version shows on the menu button; nothing is downloaded or installed by itself.
